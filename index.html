Country outlines use Natural Earth; capitals use country-data; U.S. county-equivalent search points use the 2026 U.S. Census Gazetteer (public domain); Antarctic surfaces use the Natural Earth fallback, with SCAR Antarctic Digital Database (BAS, v7.13, CC BY 4.0) detail when the vendored bundle is available. The map uses a flat circular disk with a provisional latitude-to-radius warp retained from the last accepted calibration; its North Pole and outer Antarctic rim remain fixed. The map's visual radial spacing uses a smooth, edge-anchored 12-circle display profile from 1.00× at the North Pole to 0.50× at the Ice Wall; this profile is provisional pending strict flight-data validation; the primary A–B readout measures the exact Euclidean length of the displayed flat-disk connector. The separate ADS-B empirical flight-mileage estimate remains available as a secondary reference. No globe, sphere, ellipsoid, great-circle or geodesic model is used for the primary A–B distance. ADS-B calibration candidates use only directly observed ground-speed integrals from flights with at least 98% full-flight coverage and no gap longer than 15 minutes; no missing mileage is imputed. Country, coastline, airport and capital coordinates remain location data for rendering and search only. The 60°S Restricted Line is a visual reference only. Polygon areas and perimeters remain planar. The A–B number, its drawn connector, polygon edge lengths and scale bar all use Euclidean distances in the same visibly warped flat-disk coordinate plane.
