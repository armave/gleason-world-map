Flat-disk radial calibration

R = 6,371 km (radial scale)
φ = latitude · π / 180
θ = π/2 − φ
r = R · θ
x = r · cos(λ)
y = −r · sin(λ)

The disk coordinates are positioned using r = Rθ; this controls the map layout, not the A–B distance metric. The scale bar is locally calibrated with the WGS84 geodesic at the current view centre. East longitudes run counter-clockwise, matching the Gleason sheet.

The map is displayed as a flat Gleason-style disk. The A–B numeric distance is the shortest WGS84 ellipsoidal surface distance; its straight dashed map connector carries that km label but is not the WGS84 geodesic path. Area boundary edges show projected-plane km lengths, and the completed polygon displays its planar area in km².

Country outlines use Natural Earth; capitals use country-data; U.S. county-equivalent label points use the 2026 U.S. Census Gazetteer (public domain). The map uses a flat-disk coordinate model with radial calibration r = Rθ, R = 6,371 km and outer radius πR = 20,015.09 km. A–B numeric distances use shortest WGS84 geodesics; the visible connector is always a straight dashed segment with its WGS84 km label. Polygon areas use planar shoelace geometry; each boundary edge is labelled in projected-plane km and completed polygons show their area in km².
