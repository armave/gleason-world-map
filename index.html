The map uses a flat circular disk with a smooth ADS-B-calibrated latitude-to-radius warp; its North Pole and outer Antarctic rim remain fixed. The map's visual radial spacing uses a smooth, edge-anchored 12-circle warp from 1.00× at the North Pole to 0.50× at the Ice Wall, with no hard joins; the A–B readout shows its original calibration chord, the visual chord and the separate radial multiplier. The separate ADS-B empirical flight-mileage estimate remains available as a secondary reference. No coordinate-derived track distances, globe-distance formula or geodesic library is used in the ADS-B distance calibration. Polygon areas and perimeters remain planar. The scale bar uses the visually calibrated chord geometry, without applying the separate A–B readout multiplier.
